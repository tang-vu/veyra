# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Veyra a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Demonstrate empathy, respect differing viewpoints, give and accept constructive feedback, take
responsibility for mistakes, and focus on what is best for the project and community. Unacceptable
behavior includes harassment, threats, sexualized attention, trolling, personal attacks, publishing
private information, or conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
setting.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's private contact
channel. Maintainers will investigate promptly, protect reporter privacy where possible, and may
correct, warn, temporarily ban, or permanently ban participants based on impact and pattern. Anyone
with a conflict of interest must recuse themselves from enforcement.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project in public. It is adapted from
the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
